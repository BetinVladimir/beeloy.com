# Community Rules

**Beeloy — The Conscious Business Community**

*Less routine. More freedom.*

**Document status:** Draft — pending legal review  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** To be confirmed before launch

---

## 1. Our Community

Beeloy is an open community that brings together entrepreneurs, customers, professionals, and people who believe business should make life better, not more complicated.

We believe that successful businesses are built on trust, genuine customer value, fair relationships, responsible use of technology, and continuous improvement.

Our community exists to help people share knowledge, identify problems, develop ideas, discover practical solutions, and improve the way businesses serve their customers.

Entrepreneurs and customers are equal participants in this community. Everyone has the right to express opinions, ask questions, share experiences, and contribute to constructive discussions.

**Our guiding principle: Less routine. More freedom.**

Participation is free. Support is voluntary. Trust must be earned.

## 2. Our Core Values

We ask every participant to respect the following principles.

### 2.1. Respect People and Their Time

Treat other participants with courtesy and respect.

Disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, intimidation, harassment, and humiliation are not.

### 2.2. Share Knowledge and Experience

We encourage participants to share practical experience, including mistakes, unsuccessful experiments, and lessons learned.

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. A thoughtful question or an honest description of a problem can be valuable to the entire community.

### 2.3. Focus on Solutions

We welcome criticism and discussions about problems.

Whenever possible, explain what happened, why it matters, and what could be improved.

Constructive criticism does not require proposing a solution.

### 2.4. Be Honest and Transparent

Do not knowingly publish false information, fabricate experiences, impersonate others, or misrepresent commercial relationships.

Clearly disclose relevant conflicts of interest when recommending products, companies, or services.

### 2.5. Respect Different Opinions

You may disagree with Beeloy, its founders, partners, sponsors, technologies, or community principles.

Reasoned criticism of artificial intelligence, automation, business practices, and Beeloy itself is explicitly permitted.

Agreement with Beeloy's philosophy is not a condition for expressing an opinion.

### 2.6. Technology Should Serve People

We encourage responsible automation, AI First thinking, and data-informed decisions.

However, technology must not be used to deceive, exploit, manipulate, or unnecessarily burden people.

## 3. Participation and Accounts

Public content on Beeloy.com is generally available without registration, except for resources with restricted access.

Registration is required to post comments, submit reviews, participate in discussions, send private messages, and use other interactive community features.

Users may register through supported authentication methods, including email verification, Google, or Facebook.

Each registered participant has a unique public username (handle) and a public profile URL.

Your public nickname and handle may appear alongside your comments, reviews, ideas, and other public contributions.

Your email address, when provided, is not displayed publicly.

You are responsible for activity conducted through your account, subject to applicable law.

You must not impersonate another person, organization, moderator, or Beeloy representative.

## 4. Comments and Discussions

We encourage open, thoughtful, and respectful conversations.

Participants may:

- Ask questions and request explanations.
- Express opinions and disagree with others.
- Share professional and personal experiences.
- Criticize products, services, companies, and business practices.
- Suggest improvements and alternative approaches.
- Provide evidence, references, and practical examples.

The following conduct is prohibited:

- Threats, harassment, bullying, or targeted abuse.
- Hate speech or unlawful discriminatory content.
- Disclosure of another person's private information without a lawful basis.
- Spam, repetitive unsolicited promotions, or coordinated manipulation.
- Fraudulent claims, impersonation, or deliberately fabricated evidence.
- Illegal content or instructions intended to facilitate unlawful activity.
- Malicious links, malware, phishing, or attempts to compromise platform security.
- Repeated disruption of discussions or deliberate evasion of moderation measures.

Strong criticism is not automatically a violation. Moderation should distinguish criticism of ideas or conduct from personal abuse.

## 5. Reviews and Business Reputation

Beeloy supports honest feedback as a way to improve businesses and help customers make informed decisions.

Reviews should reflect genuine experiences or clearly identified, relevant professional assessments.

When publishing a review:

- Describe your experience as accurately as possible.
- Distinguish facts from personal opinions.
- Avoid publishing unnecessary personal information.
- Disclose relevant business relationships or incentives.
- Do not fabricate transactions, experiences, or evidence.

Businesses and their representatives may respond to reviews and explain their position.

A negative review is not grounds for removal merely because it is unfavorable to a business.

Beeloy may investigate reviews suspected of being fraudulent, unlawful, manipulated, or otherwise contrary to these Rules.

Payment, sponsorship, or partnership with Beeloy does not provide preferential treatment in review moderation or reputation assessments.

## 6. Beeloy Ideas — Ideas, Questions, and Problems

Beeloy Ideas allows participants to submit ideas, questions, problems, and experiences for editorial consideration.

You do not need to provide a polished article or a complete solution.

Beeloy aims to review submissions, provide feedback, and, where appropriate, develop them into informative publications or community discussions.

### 6.1. Recognition of Original Contributions

Beeloy respects the origin and authorship of contributions.

We commit to:

- Preserving the original submission and its recorded attribution, subject to applicable retention and privacy requirements.
- Recognizing the original contributor in accordance with their chosen public attribution.
- Distinguishing the original contribution from subsequent editorial additions.
- Not knowingly presenting another person's original contribution as Beeloy's own.
- Allowing contributors to raise concerns about inaccurate representation of their submissions.

Contributors may choose public attribution by nickname, pseudonym, or anonymity, where supported.

### 6.2. Editorial Development

Beeloy may clarify, organize, research, expand, translate, and contextualize submitted material.

Editorial development must respect the original meaning and accurately distinguish the contributor's position from editorial interpretation.

Publication is not guaranteed.

Submissions may be declined, combined with existing discussions, returned for clarification, or addressed privately.

Where a submission is declined, Beeloy aims to provide a meaningful explanation.

### 6.3. Intellectual Property

Contributors retain the rights they hold in their original content, subject to the Terms of Use and applicable law.

Submitting an idea does not automatically transfer ownership of protected original material to Beeloy.

Abstract ideas, facts, and concepts may not themselves qualify for copyright protection.

The permissions necessary for Beeloy to review, edit, translate, store, and publish submissions are governed by the Terms of Use and any additional permissions obtained from contributors.

## 7. Private Messaging

All registered users may initiate private conversations with other registered users through the Beeloy Messenger.

The messenger is an official internal communication channel for the platform.

Beeloy may use it to deliver service-related messages, including responses to submissions, moderation decisions, account notices, and other operational communications.

Users must not use private messaging for:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation.
- Unsolicited mass advertising.
- Phishing, fraud, or deceptive offers.
- Repeated unwanted contact.
- Distribution of malicious or unlawful content.
- Attempts to obtain sensitive information through deception.

Users may block other participants and report inappropriate messages.

Beeloy may apply reasonable anti-spam measures and communication limits to protect participants.

Private messages are not publicly indexed or displayed.

Beeloy does not routinely make private conversations available to other users or staff. Access for safety investigations, legal compliance, or reported violations must be limited, authorized, and documented.

The messenger is not represented as end-to-end encrypted unless that functionality is explicitly implemented and disclosed.

## 8. Commercial Content and Conflicts of Interest

Entrepreneurs, experts, and businesses are welcome to participate.

Sharing professional knowledge and explaining relevant products or services is permitted when it contributes meaningfully to a discussion.

However:

- Unsolicited advertising and repetitive promotional messages are prohibited.
- Commercial relationships must be disclosed when relevant.
- Sponsored materials must be clearly identified.
- Paid endorsements must not be presented as independent personal experiences.
- Partners and sponsors must follow the same community rules as other participants.

Beeloy may publish information about its own technologies and services, but the relationship between Beeloy.com and the Beeloy company must remain transparent.

**Financial support never purchases editorial control, immunity from criticism, or preferential moderation.**

## 9. AI-Generated Content

AI tools may be used to research, translate, organize, draft, and improve content.

Participants remain responsible for the material they publish.

AI-generated content must not be used to fabricate personal experiences, reviews, sources, credentials, or evidence.

Where AI has materially contributed to content in a way relevant to its credibility, that contribution should be disclosed.

Beeloy may use AI-assisted tools to support editorial review, translation, classification, spam detection, and moderation.

Significant moderation decisions should remain subject to appropriate human oversight and applicable rights of review.

## 10. Privacy and Personal Information

Respect the privacy of other participants.

Do not publish private email addresses, telephone numbers, residential addresses, identity documents, private correspondence, or other personal information without an appropriate legal basis.

Do not publish private messages from other users without authorization or another lawful justification.

Public contributions may be visible to search engines and people outside the community.

Users should avoid including unnecessary personal or confidential business information in public posts.

Personal data is processed according to the Beeloy Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

## 11. Moderation

Beeloy maintains moderation procedures to protect participants, support constructive discussion, and comply with applicable law.

Authorized moderators may:

- Review reported content.
- Request clarification or corrections.
- Hide or remove comments and other content.
- Restrict access to specific resources.
- Temporarily limit account functionality.
- Suspend or terminate accounts in serious or repeated cases.
- Restore content or access when a moderation decision is reversed.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and based on identifiable rules.

Criticism of Beeloy, its partners, or its technologies must not be treated as a violation merely because it is critical.

Where required by law, affected users will receive an explanation of moderation decisions and information about available complaint or appeal procedures.

## 12. Reporting and Appeals

Participants may report content, messages, or behavior that they believe violates these Rules or applicable law.

Reports should include enough information to identify the relevant content and explain the concern.

Beeloy will provide accessible reporting mechanisms and review reports according to their nature and urgency.

Users may challenge moderation decisions through the available internal complaint procedure, where applicable.

Reports and appeals will be handled without discrimination or preferential treatment based on sponsorship, partnership, or commercial relationships.

Additional procedures and rights required by applicable law, including the EU Digital Services Act, will be respected.

## 13. Community Safety

Beeloy may take reasonable measures to prevent abuse, spam, fraudulent activity, account compromise, and threats to participants.

Such measures may include rate limits, automated detection, temporary restrictions, and human review.

Users must not attempt to bypass security controls, abuse platform infrastructure, or interfere with the availability of services.

## 14. No Guarantee of Professional Results

Community discussions, reviews, ideas, and articles are provided for information, education, and exchange of experience.

They do not automatically constitute individualized legal, financial, tax, medical, or other regulated professional advice.

Participants should independently assess recommendations and seek qualified assistance where appropriate.

Beeloy does not guarantee particular commercial or financial results from applying information shared through the community.

Nothing in these Rules excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 15. Changes to These Rules

Beeloy may update these Community Rules as the platform develops or legal requirements change.

The current version and effective date will be published on the website.

Material changes will be communicated to registered users where appropriate or legally required.

Previous versions may be retained for transparency and accountability.

## 16. Contact and Community Governance

Beeloy welcomes questions, suggestions, criticism, and proposals for improving these Rules.

Registered users may contact the administration through the internal messenger or designated support channels.

Formal legal and privacy contact information will be published in the relevant legal notices.

The community's governance should remain transparent, accountable, and open to constructive improvement.
